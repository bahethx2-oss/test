Transformer Efficiency in Climate Modeling

Recent work shows that transformer architectures outperform recurrent models in long-horizon climate prediction. The attention mechanism reduces error accumulation in multi-decade forecasts.

Key Finding

Our experiments demonstrate that 8-head attention layers achieve a 23% reduction in prediction error compared to LSTM baselines on the CMIP6 dataset.

Foundation

This work builds directly on the transformer architecture introduced by Vaswani et al. (2017), "Attention Is All You Need" (NeurIPS 30, 5998-6008), which established the core attention mechanism we adapt for climate data.
